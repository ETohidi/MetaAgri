MetaAgri

    MetaAgri, a non-commercial demo. Above the farm, real figures come from official open sources and carry their source, date and licence on each page (real figures: see web/data/LICENSES.md). The farms are fictional: simulated numbers appear only for them, at Farm and Field level, always marked Simulated and never mixed with real ones. Map data: Natural Earth (public domain), German states from deutschlandGeoJSON (Unlicense); the Oder river and Oderbruch outline simplified by hand. Font: Inter (SIL OFL).
